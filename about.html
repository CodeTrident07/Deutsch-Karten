Praanav

Hi, I'm Praanav. I'm a student living in Germany, and I built DeutschKarten.

Why I made it

I started learning German at a language school at the end of 2025, online at first and then in person after I moved to Germany in March. I went from A1 to C1 in about eight months and finished with an average grade of 1,4.

DeutschKarten started as what I used to study for those courses. I shared it because the things that helped me, like always learning a noun together with its article, could help anyone preparing for a course, an exam or a move to Germany. Building it also taught me a lot about making websites.

What's on the site

  • Flashcards A1 to C1, with spaced repetition
  • Wördle A new German word every day
  • Sprint Spell as many words as you can in 30 seconds
  • Der/Die/Das Practise noun genders
  • Spelling Type the German word from the English
  • Phrases Everyday expressions and idioms
  • Präpositionen Verbs with fixed prepositions
  • Starke Verben Irregular verb forms
  • Leaderboard See how you compare

Free, no ads

DeutschKarten is free and has no ads. Your progress is saved in your own browser, not on a server.
